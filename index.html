🌌 Celestron NexStar 8SE

Quick Alignment & Handset Guide

Phase 1: Physical Setup

  1. Stability: Set up the tripod on level ground. Use vibration suppression pads if available.
  2. Mounting: Attach the mount arm. Slide the Optical Tube Assembly (OTA) into the rail and tighten the knob securely.
  3. Balance: Move the OTA slightly forward (~1 inch) in the dovetail saddle to create a slight "fore-end imbalance."
  4. Power: Plug into a dedicated power source. Avoid AA batteries to prevent voltage drops.
  5. Leveling: Turn the scope on, move tube to horizontal, turn off. Adjust tripod legs until perfectly level.

Phase 2: Pre-Alignment Calibration

1. Align the Finder

Crucial: Your finder must point exactly where the telescope points.

  • Find a distant object (pole, tree, or bright star).
  • Center the object perfectly using the main telescope eyepiece.
  • Adjust finder knobs until the object is also centered in the finder.

2. Software Data Entry

  • Power on SCROLL UP/DOWN select Two-Star Align ENTER.
  • Review Time/Location: ENTER to accept or BACK to edit.

Phase 3: The Two-Star Alignment Process

Note: Use a long focal length eyepiece (e.g., 25mm) for these steps.

Step 1: Select Star 1
When SELECT STAR 1 appears, use SCROLL UP/DOWN to choose a bright star ENTER.
Step 2: Center Star 1
Use direction arrows to slew the telescope and center the star in the finderscope ENTER. Then, center the star in the eyepiece ALIGN.
HINT: Press the MOTOR SPEED button to select a slower speed (1-9) for fine centering.
Step 3: Select and Center Star 2
Choose a second star (ideally 40°–60° away from the first). Slew and center in the finderscope ENTER. Then, center in the eyepiece using the same approach angle ALIGN.
Alignment Complete
The display will read “Align Successful” and tracking motors will engage.

🕹️ Handset Operation Guide

Button Logic

MENU: Enters the main system settings. Press again to exit.

ENTER: Confirms a selection or proceeds to the next step.

BACK: Undoes a selection or returns to the previous menu.

Slew Arrows: Manually moves the telescope (Up, Down, Left, Right).

MOTOR SPEED: Changes the slew rate (1 = Slowest, 9 = Fastest). Use for precision centering.

🚀 Pro Tips for Maximum Accuracy

The "Doughnut" Centering Method
Slightly defocus the star until it becomes a soft, blurry circle. It is much easier to judge if a large circle is centered than a tiny dot. Refocus just before aligning.
The "Up-and-Right" Rule
To beat gear backlash, always make your final movements UP and RIGHT. If you overshoot, move the star back to the bottom-left quadrant and approach again moving Up and Right.
Advanced: Anti-Backlash Settings
Menu Scope Set-Up Anti-Backlash. Slew to a distant object during the day and adjust Azimuth/Altitude values until movement is smooth.
